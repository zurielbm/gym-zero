01Principles

Five rules that decide every screen. When a layout question comes up during the build, the answer is whichever option follows more of these.

Data is the decorationNo illustrations, no gradients, no glass. The big numerals — streak, volume, PRs — are the visual interest. If a screen looks empty, make the numbers bigger, don't add ornament.
One accent, spent carefullyLime marks exactly three things: the primary action, the active nav item, and achievement (PRs, completed days). Everything else is ink/muted/hairline. If lime appears more than ~3 times on a screen, something is misclassified.
Rules, not cardsContent is grouped by 1px hairlines and whitespace, never boxed. The only filled surfaces are inputs and the two floating elements (rest bar, scan key). This is also why Ignite is the cheapest CSS of the three directions.
Two typefaces, two jobsThe condensed display face (Anton on this page) does numerals and screen titles only. Inter does everything readable: body, inputs, buttons, food names, notes. Nothing set in Anton is ever longer than ~3 words.
Flat and fastZero border-radius on structure (inputs get 0–2px), no shadows, and motion under 200ms. The app should feel like flipping scoreboard digits, not like floating panels.

02Design system

Color tokens

Nine tokens total. --lime is the only saturated color; --danger appears exclusively on destructive actions (discard workout, delete entry).

--surface#131316 · inputs, rest bar
--track#1e1e21 · empty bars

--muted#82827e
--ghost#3a3a3e · outline numerals
--lime#d8f34f · on-lime ink #101204

Contrast: ink on bg 17.9:1, muted on bg 5.6:1, lime on bg 13.4:1, on-lime ink on lime 11.9:1 — everything clears WCAG AA at the sizes used, including the 8px tracking labels.

Typography

Display: Anton (free, one 400 weight, ~48KB woff2, self-hosted). Body: Inter (already the app font — no change). Anton's digits are effectively monospaced at display sizes, which keeps the workout clock and set numbers from jittering.

The caps rule: Anton and the tracked labels are all-caps; Inter body copy is sentence case. Food names, routine exercise lists, notes, and machine nicknames stay lowercase-readable — caps are for structure, never for content you scan repeatedly.

Shape & space

Radius 0 everywhere except inputs (2px). Hairlines are 1px --hairline; progress tracks 3px tall on mobile, 4px on desktop. Spacing scale: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 40. Touch targets stay ≥ 44px even where the visual is a 3px bar (padded hit areas).

06Density & edge cases

The risk flagged in round one: bold athletic styles fall apart on dense, form-heavy screens. Ignite's answer is a strict altitude split.

The split: Anton + caps live only at the screen altitude (titles, stat numerals, nav). Everything at the row altitude — exercise lists, food entries, form fields, notes — is plain Inter at 13–14px, sentence case, exactly like a normal app. A dense screen in Ignite is just a normal readable list under a loud header, so density can't break it.

  • Long names ("Seated Alternating Dumbbell Curl", "Chest press by the windows"): row altitude → Inter, wraps to 2 lines, never truncates mid-word. Anton never gets long strings, so its tight setting is never stress-tested.
  • Forms (quick-add food, map-new-machine): boxed --surface inputs with hairline borders, labels in the tracked 10px style. Shown working in the Fuel desktop mock above.
  • Zero states: the outlined "ghost numeral" pattern (kit, above) keeps empty screens on-brand instead of blank.
  • Confirmations: destructive actions (discard workout) get the danger color and a full-width ghost button, never lime — lime always means "go".

07Build notes — deltas to the phased plan

The 5-phase plan from round one stands. Ignite adds these specifics:

Phase A
  • Self-host Anton (single 400 woff2, ~48KB) with font-display: swap and a local()-first stack; Inter stays as-is. Token sheet is the 9 colors + 2 face variables above.
  • Tab bar becomes 5-slot with the center scan key; the fabScreens logic and .fab.raised hack in App.tsx are deleted.
  • Rest toast → docked rest bar component, rendered between screen and tab bar in the shell.
Phase B
  • 12 components as shown in the kit. Radius/shadow tokens go to 0 — a lot of today's CSS simply gets deleted (Ignite is net-negative lines).
  • Numeral count-in, bar fill, and PR flash as three shared keyframe utilities gated on prefers-reduced-motion.
Phase C
  • Screen order unchanged (Home → Workout first). Workout screen additionally swaps the set-check button to the 26px lime square and moves "last time" under the table as a label line.
Phase D
  • Desktop = top nav + hairline-ruled columns (mocks above). Set entry gets keyboard flow: Tab lb→reps, Enter logs the set and starts rest, ⌘K exercise switcher.
Phase E
  • Extra check for Ignite specifically: caps-label legibility pass on a real phone at arm's length (the 10px tracked labels are the most fragile element), plus Anton fallback rendering check (Arial Narrow) before the font loads.

08Q&A — decisions before Phase A starts

1. Display font: Anton (as shown) — good enough, or upgrade?

This whole page is set in Anton — free (OFL), one weight, tiny file. Alternatives: Archivo Black (slightly friendlier, less condensed), or a licensed grotesk like Druk (the actual Nike-poster face, ~$250+/style).

Why I'm asking: the font is this design — swapping it later re-tunes every numeral size. Judge Anton by scrolling the mocks above: if the headers and clocks already feel right to you, we lock it and pay nothing.

2. Confirm the accent: lime #d8f34f?

Everything above uses lime. Same system works with signal orange #ff5c1f (hotter, more aggressive) or iced mint #7df0c8 (a nod to the current app's green). One-line token change now, a re-review of every screen later.

Why I'm asking: with a one-accent system the accent is ~80% of the personality. Lime photographs "electric/energy", orange "intensity", mint "clean". This is taste — only you can pick it.

3. Scan key in the center of the tab bar — keep it?

(a) Yes — scan is the app's signature action, give it the permanent center slot (as mocked) · (b) No — keep today's floating button on workout screens only, tab bar stays 4 slots.

Why I'm asking: it's the one navigation change in an otherwise visual redesign. Center slot makes scanning reachable from Food/History too and kills the FAB-vs-rest-timer collision hack, but it permanently spends the most valuable pixel on screen. Depends on how often you actually scan mid-gym vs. start from a routine.

4. "Evening, Zuriel." — add a name field to Settings?

The mocks greet you by name, but Settings has no name field today — the current app says "Good evening 👋". Options: (a) add an optional name in Settings (tiny scope add, one field + one migration) · (b) drop the name, greeting stays "Evening." with the lime period.

Why I'm asking: it's the only thing in these mocks the data layer can't render today, and the no-name fallback changes the Home header's typographic balance — better to decide now than discover it in Phase C. Honestly, "Evening." alone looks pretty strong too.
